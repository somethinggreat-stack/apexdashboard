Apex Team Chat

The desktop app for the Apex team — messages, files and notifications, right on your PC.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 / 11 · ~2 MB installer

How to install

1Run the downloaded Apex Team Chat setup.
2Windows may show “Windows protected your PC.” Click More info → Run anyway — it’s safe, just not code-signed.
3Open Apex Team Chat from your Start menu and sign in with your Apex email.
Tip: right-click the app icon in your taskbar → Pin to taskbar, and enable notifications so you never miss a message.
